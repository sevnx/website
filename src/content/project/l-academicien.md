## Presentation

"Le Petit Académicien Sans Faute" is a web platform designed to help young students assess and improve their French language skills. Developed in partnership between our university and the Académie française, the platform features a series of quizzes targeting specific linguistic concepts, ranging from grammar to vocabulary.

Each concept is accompanied by a detailed write-up with practical examples, enabling students to identify and address their weaknesses.

The platform has not yet been finalized, as such it is not yet available to the public.

## Key responsibilities

- Designed and implemented both the **frontend and backend** of the exercise system, including quiz rendering, answer validation, and progress tracking
- Built the **content management system** with all necessary features to provide a smooth experience for content creators
- Presented the final result of our work in front of representatives from the **Academie francaise**
