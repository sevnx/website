## Description

L'Académicien is a web platform for young students to practise French through quizzes, explanations, and examples. I worked on the exercise system and content management during a university team project in partnership with the Académie française.

I built the quiz and content management interfaces in React and TypeScript, with PHP answer validation and progress tracking backed by MySQL. We presented our work to representatives of the Académie française; the platform was still unfinished at handover.
