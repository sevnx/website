## Description

Farsight is a League of Legends analysis tool for reviewing ranked matches and tracking performance across a season. Players can browse their match history, filter stats by champion, role, or matchup, and compare their performance with the opponents they faced.

Using Riot's match and timeline data, it connects fights and objectives to gold swings and shows how leads formed and where matches turned. Detected strengths and mistakes link to the numbers and timestamps behind them, with Map Replay for inspecting those moments. Players can also add notes to their matches and track what they want to improve.
